# Ash, the sister, keeper of the signal fire at the top of the mountain. The end of the story:
# what you say to her decides the ending (see endings.txt).

Ash: ...You found my notes? All of them? Then you know everything already.

--- !ash_answered
Ash: ...You? You climbed all this way?
You: You didn't come back, Ash. Three winters.
Ash: I know. I'm sorry. I found this place, and the fire. Lost climbers see it from the dark and find their way up.
Ash: Every time I meant to leave, there was someone else down there in the dark.
? Come home with me.           -> home
? Then I'll stay with you.     -> stay
? I understand.                -> letgo

Ash: ...You're right. Someone else can learn to keep the fire. Let's go home.

-> end
= stay
Ash: Two keepers, then. The mountain won't know what hit it.

-> end
= letgo
Ash: Tell them I'm all right. Tell them the light's on, and that I'll keep it on.
set ash_letgo
set ash_answered
-> end
