# Moss, keeper of the Archive at the top of the ruins, where Ash's chalk bag waits on the top shelf.

--- !wall_climb
Moss: ...A visitor. The mushrooms told me someone was coming. They are terrible at secrets.
Moss: You have the look of someone following footprints.
? I'm looking for my sister, Ash.    -> ash
? What is this place?                -> place
= ash
Moss: Ash. Yes. She sat where you are standing and read every map I had.
Moss: She left something with me. Her chalk bag. She said, if anyone comes after me, give it to them.
Moss: It's on the top shelf. I am too old for shelves.
set heard_chalk
-> end
= place
Moss: The Archive. Everything anyone ever wrote about the mountain is here. Most of it is wrong.
Moss: There's a chalk bag on the top shelf. With chalk on your hands, a wall is a ladder. Take it. It's no use to a book.

--- !wall_climb
Moss: The top shelf. Climb the lower ones first.

--- wall_climb
Moss: The chalk took to your hands. Good. Rock remembers who it held.
Moss: Far up, past the summit, there is a ledge where the wind blows so hard that nothing holds. Not hands, not rope.
? Is Ash up there?      -> up
? Thank you, Moss.      -> end
= up
Moss: Climbers go up the mountain to find something. Some of them find it. Some of them stay with it.
Moss: Go and see.

--- wall_climb !asked_page
Moss: One more thing, since you're climbing. My oldest book is missing its last page.
Moss: The wind took it, years ago, up past the Summit. Into the Frost Caves, I expect. Everything cold ends up there.
Moss: If you find it, I would very much like to read how it ends.
set asked_page

--- moss_page !gave_page
Moss: The last page! The ink's run, but... yes. Yes. So that's how it ends.
Moss: Take this. A scholar's robe. It's no use to me: I never go anywhere.
take item moss_page
give outfit scholar
set gave_page

--- wall_climb
Moss: Go on. The books will still be here. They always are.
