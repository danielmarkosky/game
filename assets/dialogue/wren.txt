# Wren, an old climber who lives at the bottom of The Cave, by where you start.

--- !climbing_pick
Wren: Oh! A climber, down here? Nobody comes this way anymore.
You: I'm trying to get out. Up, I guess.
Wren: Up is the only way out of this hole. Mind the spikes.
Wren: That rope of yours only bites metal, you know. See the blocks with the yellow mark? Aim at one, shoot, and hold on.
Wren: Bare rock is another story. You'd need a climbing pick for that.
Wren: I lost mine down the corridor, years ago. If you find it, it's yours.

--- !climbing_pick
Wren: Still here? The corridor's that way. My pick's out there somewhere, past the second flag.

--- climbing_pick
Wren: Is that my old pick? Well, look at you!
Wren: With that, your rope sticks to rock, not just metal. You can swing from anything now.
You: Thanks. I'll take good care of it.
Wren: Oh, and keep your eyes up. There's something shiny stuck in the ceiling, just along from here. Always wanted it, never could reach.

--- climbing_pick
Wren: Up and out, climber. And don't look down.
