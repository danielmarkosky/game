# Bramble, who keeps the Greenhouse in the Hook Garden, and races you to the bottom of Curtains.

--- !asked_race_garden
Bramble: Mind the seedlings! Oh, a climber. I can tell by the rope burn.
Bramble: I'm Bramble. I keep the Greenhouse, and I'm the fastest thing in this garden.
Bramble: A girl with a green scarf tried to beat me once. Twice, actually. She didn't.
Bramble: Bet you can't get from here to the bottom of Curtains in forty seconds.
set asked_race_garden
? You're on.         -> race
? Another time.      -> end
= race
Bramble: Ready? Go!
race garden

--- won_garden !paid_garden
Bramble: You did it? In forty? Well, a bet's a bet.
Bramble: Here. My spare hat and apron. You've earned them.
give outfit gardener
set paid_garden

--- !won_garden
Bramble: Back for another go? The bottom of Curtains, forty seconds. The clock starts when you say.
? Go!          -> race
? Not now.     -> end
= race
race garden
