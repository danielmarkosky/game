Fen: Up on the mountain there's a light called the Lantern. For as long as anyone remembers, it held the stars up there.
Fen: A keeper tended it. An old woman, hard as rope. Then one night it went dark, and nobody's been up since.
Fen: The old line runs up there, from the station over yonder. The cart won't run till the four beacons burn again.
Fen: One out at the end of every way from this fire. East along the road. Down the sinkhole.
Fen: Those two you can do now. The caves off the lookout need a longer rope than yours. The sky road needs hands that can hold rock.
Fen: Rest first. The bench by the fire keeps your place.

Fen: East along the road, the Hook Garden. Down the sinkhole, the Hollows. Either first.
Fen: What you find at the end of one opens another.

--- long_rope
Fen: A long rope. Good. The Frost Caves, off the lookout, have a gap only that reaches across.
Fen: And the waterfall in the lake hides more than it shows. I'd look there again, if I could reach as far as you.

--- wall_climb
Fen: Chalk on your hands. Then the sky road's yours: up the top of the lookout, there's a wall nothing else gets you up.

--- dog
Fen: And who's this, then? He won't leave your heel.
You: Pip. It says so on his collar. And I think... I think he's mine.
Fen: Then he's been looking for you. Dogs have good noses. Gems, too, I'd bet. Watch where he points.

--- stopwatch
Fen: Where did you find that? It's very old. And it's ticking.
You: Behind the waterfall. Where the falls begin. It was just floating there.
You: It's my Gran's. She used to time me, climbing. Again! Faster!
Fen: The keeper's. Of course. Then you know whose granddaughter climbs my lookout every night.
Fen: Time your runs with it. She'd want you to.
